1. What am I actually trying to do?

I am trying to make a side project that will eventually go on my resume. I want to show that I can work with AI in an RL environment, so I am creating an AI that can get really good at math. I plan on doing this by using a SFT and GRPO algorithm to train the AI on first the GSM8K dataset, and then work up to the NiminaMath-CoT dataset. I want to have a working AI, but also personally understand every part of it, and be able to show it off on a resume. 

2. What are the milestones of functionality do you want. 

MVP: Should be able to finish a full training on GSM8K dataset.

v1: Should be able to get a much higher accuracy on test questions on GSM8K questions after GRPO training compared to original model.

v2: Added website that shows employers difference between model accuracy

v3: Newly trained model that is trained to solve GSM8K problems, NuminaMath-CoT problems, and other difficult datasets. 

v4: Actually does well on these datasets, similar to top of the line math models out there.

v5: Comparison between all models made on website, user can select preset questions or enter their own on the website. 

Later (not a priority): a model that learns from user questions and answers in the websites
